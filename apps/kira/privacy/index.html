Introduction

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Our guiding principle is simple: we only collect the minimum data needed to run and improve Kira; we never sell your data. By using Kira (the “Service”), you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy (“Policy”).

What Does This Privacy Policy Cover?

This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you access or use:

  • Kira! mobile applications (including iOS and any future platforms)
  • Our websites, networks, or widgets linking to this Policy
  • Any other services we provide that reference this Policy

What Information Do We Collect?

1. Information You Provide

Kira! does not require account registration. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name or phone number. If you contact support or sign up for a newsletter, we will store your email address solely to respond to you.

2. Analytics Data

To understand how Kira! is used, we collect anonymized, aggregated analytics (e.g., session length, feature usage). This data never includes card images, personal identifiers, or sensitive content. You can opt-out of analytics in the app’s settings.

3. Cookies & Technical Data (Website Only)

Our website may set essential cookies (e.g., for authentication). These cookies are purely functional and not used for tracking across sites.

How Do We Use the Information We Collect?

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve Kira!’s features
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Monitor aggregate usage metrics and performance
  • Develop new features and services

We do not use your data for profiling beyond the purposes listed above.

Sharing or Disclosing Information

We do not sell user data. We only share or disclose information:

  • With trusted third-party service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, advertising) bound by confidentiality agreements
  • To comply with legal obligations (court orders, law enforcement requests)
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Kira, our users, or others
  • With your explicit consent

Any shared analytics data is aggregated or de-identified.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & Correction – Request a copy of the data we hold about you and ask us to correct inaccuracies.
  • Deletion (“Right to be Forgotten”) – Ask us to erase personal data we store.
  • Restriction & Objection – Limit or object to certain data uses (e.g., analytics, advertising).
  • Portability – Receive a transferable copy of your data, when applicable.
  • Withdraw Consent – Opt-out of analytics or marketing at any time via in-app settings or by emailing us.

We will honor your request within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal requirements.

Children’s Privacy

Kira is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encryption in transit, secure servers, limited employee access) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data.

International Data Transfers

If you access Kira from outside the United States, your data may be processed in the U.S. or other countries. We take appropriate measures to ensure an adequate level of data protection.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app or on our website. Continued use of Kira after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

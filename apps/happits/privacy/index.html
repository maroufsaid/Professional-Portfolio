Happits Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2024

At Happits, we believe your habit-building journey should be completely private. We take your privacy seriously and have designed our app to operate without collecting any personal data. All your habit data stays on your device, and we have no way to access it.

What Does This Privacy Policy Cover?

This policy covers how Happits handles (or rather, doesn't handle) your data across our iOS application and any related services where a link to this policy is displayed.

What Information Do We Collect?

Simply put: we don't collect any personal information. Here's how Happits works:

  • All habit data is stored locally on your device within the app's secure container
  • Our AI feature works completely offline using on-device processing
  • We don't require registration or account creation
  • We don't use analytics or tracking systems
  • We don't use cookies
  • We don't collect crash reports or usage statistics

Data Storage and Security

Your habit data is stored exclusively on your device within the app's secure container. The only way this data leaves your device is through:

  • Your own iCloud backup (if enabled)
  • Manual export features you initiate

AI Features

Our habit suggestion feature:

  • Uses on-device machine learning
  • Works completely offline
  • Processes all data locally on your device
  • Never sends data to external servers

Children's Privacy

Happits does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, as we don't collect any personal information at all. Parents have full control over their children's use of the app through iOS's built-in parental controls.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there's no need to:

  • Request access to your data (it's all on your device)
  • Request deletion of your data (just delete the app)
  • Opt-out of data collection (we don't collect any)
  • Request data portability (use the app's export feature)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The latest version will always be available in the app and on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: instaliciousapp@gmail.com

Our Privacy Commitment:

Happits is built on the principle that your habits are your business. We don't need or want your personal data. Your information stays on your device, and that's exactly how we believe it should be.

Notice for California Users

Under California Civil Code Section 1798.83, California residents may request certain information regarding our disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. However, we do not collect or share any personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We honor Do Not Track signals from your browser, although this is not relevant as we do not track users in any way.
